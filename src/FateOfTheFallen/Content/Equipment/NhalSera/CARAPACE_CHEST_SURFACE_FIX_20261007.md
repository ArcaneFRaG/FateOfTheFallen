# Carapace Chest Surface Fix

User feedback on the previous hybrid pass:
- chest accents still did not match the intended mockup;
- chest accents visibly floated in front of the player.

This revision makes three specific changes:

1. The native Eventide broad purple channels are toned down again so the
   shoulder blocks stop dominating the look.
2. The chest overlay is rebuilt as a lower-profile surface-fit layer:
   - 6 triangles total:
     - 4 triangles for the two larger faceted chest accents
     - 2 triangles for the side shards
3. The chest triangles are placed slightly INSIDE the measured front surface of
   HeavyPlate5, then lifted by only a tiny amount, so they should read as
   inlaid/flush rather than floating.

The shoulder overlay remains only two very thin outer-edge lines.
